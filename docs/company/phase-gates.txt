PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Concrete criteria for moving between phases. Hit the numbers, move forward.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PHASE 1 → PHASE 2: Sales Bootcamp → System Maturity
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Move when ALL are true:

[ ] 3+ paying clients on monthly plans
[ ] MRR ≥ €300
[ ] At least 2 clients live for 30+ days
[ ] At least 1 written testimonial
[ ] Onboarding takes < 3 hours (your time)
[ ] 10+ sales conversations completed
[ ] Can explain the product in under 60 seconds without notes
[ ] No critical bugs in production

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PHASE 2 → PHASE 3: System Maturity → Vertical Expansion
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Move when ALL are true:

[ ] 8+ paying clients
[ ] MRR ≥ €800
[ ] Churn rate < 15% monthly
[ ] Onboarding takes < 1.5 hours (your time)
[ ] 3+ testimonials / case studies
[ ] Billing is automated (no manual invoicing)
[ ] Documented playbook for sales, onboarding, and support
[ ] At least 1 client on Premium tier
[ ] At least 2 inbound enquiries (people coming to you)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PHASE 3 ENTRY: Vertical Expansion
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

You should have:
- Confidence in sales conversations
- Recurring revenue covering basic costs
- Documented processes for everything
- Proof (case studies, testimonials, data)

Actions:
- Research accountant / professional services pain points
- Adapt AI Audit offering for new verticals
- Build new sales materials for non-physio audiences
- Test outreach to 2–3 accountancy firms
- Price AI Audit at €1,500+ (you have proof now)
