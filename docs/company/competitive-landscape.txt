INDIRECT COMPETITORS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

These aren't doing exactly what we do, but a prospect might compare us to them.

Category: Generic chatbot tools (Tidio, Intercom, Drift)
----------------------------------------------
Why prospects consider them: They've heard of them. Big brand names.
Why we win: They're generic. We're built specifically for Irish service businesses. No setup required from the client. We handle everything.

Category: Web agencies offering "AI add-ons"
----------------------------------------------
Why prospects consider them: Their existing web agency offers to bolt on a chatbot.
Why we win: We specialise in this. It's not an afterthought. Our system is purpose-built, not a WordPress plugin.

Category: DIY tools (ChatGPT, etc.)
----------------------------------------------
Why prospects consider them: "Can't I just use ChatGPT?"
Why we win: ChatGPT doesn't sit on your website, capture leads, integrate with your booking system, or run 24/7 without you touching it.

OUR POSITIONING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

We are NOT:
- A chatbot company
- A web agency
- A generic SaaS tool

We ARE:
- An AI automation partner for Irish service businesses
- Vertical-specific (physio first, then professional services)
- Done-for-you (client does almost nothing)
- Focused on revenue impact (leads captured, bookings made)

One-liner when asked:
"We help physio clinics capture more bookings from their website using AI — it runs 24/7, answers patient questions, and sends you leads automatically."
