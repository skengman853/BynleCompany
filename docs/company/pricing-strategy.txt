Bynle Pricing Strategy

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
1. PRODUCT TIERS – Bynle Assist
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Starter – €79/month
----------------------------------------------
- AI chat widget on website
- Trained on clinic FAQs (up to 20 Q&As)
- Lead capture (name, email, phone, message)
- Email notifications for new leads
- Basic branding (logo + colours)
- Email support

Best for: Solo physios, small clinics testing the waters.

Professional – €129/month
----------------------------------------------
- Everything in Starter
- Booking integration (Cliniko, Jane, or calendar link)
- Up to 50 Q&As
- After-hours auto-responses
- Weekly lead summary report
- Priority email support

Best for: Busy clinics that want bookings handled automatically.

Premium – €179/month
----------------------------------------------
- Everything in Professional
- Multi-location support
- Custom conversation flows
- Monthly performance review call (15 min)
- Dashboard access with analytics
- Phone support

Best for: Multi-physio practices wanting full service.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
2. SETUP FEES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Phase 1 (now): No setup fee.
→ Removing friction to get first 3–5 clients. Testimonials are worth more than a one-time fee.

Phase 2 (5+ clients): Introduce €199 one-time setup fee.

Phase 3: Setup fee €299–€499 depending on complexity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
3. CONSULTING / HIGH-TICKET SERVICES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

AI Audit – €1,000–€2,000
----------------------------------------------
- Full review of a business's workflows
- Identify where AI/automation saves time or money
- Deliverable: Written report with recommendations + ROI estimates
- Scope: 1–2 discovery calls, workflow mapping, 5–10 page report
- Timeline: 1–2 weeks

Implementation – €3,000–€10,000+
----------------------------------------------
- Build and deploy custom AI automation based on audit
- Simple (1 workflow): €3,000
- Medium (2–3 workflows): €5,000–€7,000
- Complex (full system): €8,000–€10,000+
- Always quote per-project, not hourly
- Always scope after the audit, never before

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
4. DISCOUNTS & INCENTIVES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Annual: Pay yearly = 2 months free (~17% off). Only offer if client asks.

Referral: Client refers a clinic that signs up = 1 month free for both.

Early adopter: First 5 clients get "founding client" rate locked for 12 months. Use as urgency in sales.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
5. PRICE TESTING PLAN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Clients 1–5: Lead with €99/month (Professional). If nobody flinches, you're too cheap.
Clients 5–10: Test €129/month as default. Offer €99 only if they hesitate.
Phase 2: Introduce all three tiers. Let Professional be the obvious choice.

Rule: Never discount without removing something. Lower price = fewer features.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
6. WHEN TO RAISE PRICES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Raise when:
- 5+ paying clients
- 2+ testimonials
- Onboarding under 2 hours
- Getting inbound interest

Existing clients keep their rate for 12 months. New clients get the new rate.
