Bynle Sales Playbook – Physio Clinics (Phase 1)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
1. TARGET PROFILE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Who we're selling to:
- Physiotherapy clinics in Dublin
- 1–5 physios on staff
- Have a website (even a basic one)
- Use online booking (Cliniko, Jane, PhysioTrack, or manual)
- Busy enough to miss calls/enquiries

Ideal contact:
- Clinic owner or practice manager
- Usually the senior physio

Red flags (skip these):
- No website at all
- Just opened (no patient flow yet)
- Already have a full-time receptionist and are happy with it

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
2. OUTREACH METHODS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Method A – Cold Email
----------------------------------------------
Subject: Quick question about [Clinic Name]

Hi [Name],

I was looking at [Clinic Name]'s website and had a quick question — when someone visits your site outside of hours, what happens to that enquiry?

We work with physio clinics in Dublin to make sure no lead slips through — using a simple AI assistant that answers questions, captures details, and books appointments automatically.

Would you be open to a 10-minute call this week to see if it's relevant?

Cheers,
[Your Name]
Bynle

Method B – Cold Call Script
----------------------------------------------
"Hi, is that [Name]? My name's [Your Name] from Bynle.

I'll be quick — we help physio clinics in Dublin capture more bookings from their website, especially outside of hours. We use a simple AI assistant that sits on your site, answers patient questions, and feeds leads straight to you.

I'm not trying to sell you anything on this call — I just wanted to ask: do you find you're missing enquiries that come in after hours or when you're with patients?"

[Let them talk. Listen. Ask follow-ups.]

"Would it make sense to jump on a quick 15-minute Zoom so I can show you what it looks like? No pressure at all."

Method C – Walk-In / Drop-In
----------------------------------------------
Best for clinics within driving distance.
Bring: A one-page flyer or your phone with a demo ready.

Script:
"Hi, I'm [Name] from Bynle — we help physio clinics capture more bookings using AI on their website. I was in the area and wanted to drop this off for [Owner Name]. Is there a good time to chat with them for 5 minutes?"

Goal: Get the owner's name and email. Follow up same day.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
3. DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (For Calls/Demos)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

- How do new patients typically find you?
- What happens when someone visits your website outside hours?
- Do you get a lot of repeat questions (pricing, availability, location)?
- How do you currently handle bookings — phone, email, online?
- Have you ever felt like you're losing leads because you can't respond fast enough?
- How much time do you or your staff spend on admin per day?

Listen for:
- "We miss calls when we're with patients"
- "People email and we get back to them next day"
- "We don't have a receptionist"
- "Our website doesn't really do much"

These are buying signals.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
4. DEMO STRUCTURE (15 mins)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Minute 0–2: Rapport
- "Thanks for jumping on. Before I show you anything, can I ask a couple of quick questions?"

Minute 2–5: Discovery
- Use questions from Section 3
- Identify their top 1–2 pain points

Minute 5–10: Show the product
- Screen share a live demo widget
- Show it answering a real physio question
- Show it capturing a lead
- Show it suggesting a booking
- "This runs 24/7 on your site. No extra staff needed."

Minute 10–13: Tie back to their pain
- "You mentioned you miss after-hours enquiries — this catches every single one."
- "You said you answer the same questions daily — this handles that automatically."

Minute 13–15: Close or next step
- "We're offering this at €99/month with setup included. Want to get it live on your site this week?"
- If not ready: "No problem. Can I send you a quick summary and follow up Friday?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
5. OBJECTION HANDLING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

"It's too expensive"
→ "What's one new patient worth to you? If this captures even one extra booking a month, it's paid for itself."

"I need to think about it"
→ "Of course. What specifically would help you decide? Can I check back Thursday?"

"We already have a website / booking system"
→ "This sits on top of what you already have. It doesn't replace anything, it just makes sure visitors actually convert."

"I don't trust AI / chatbots"
→ "This isn't a generic chatbot — it's trained specifically on your clinic's info. Want me to show you a quick example?"

"We're too busy right now"
→ "That's exactly why this helps — it handles the enquiries so you don't have to. Setup takes about a day on our end, minimal input from you."

"I need to talk to my partner"
→ "Makes sense. Would a one-page summary help? Can we book a follow-up for next week?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
6. FOLLOW-UP CADENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Day 0: Initial outreach (email/call/walk-in)
Day 1: If no reply, short follow-up email
Day 3: Second follow-up — add a small insight or stat
Day 7: Final follow-up — "No worries if the timing isn't right"

After demo:
Day 0: Send summary email with next steps
Day 2: "Any questions from the demo?"
Day 5: "Would you like to get started this week?"
Day 10: "I'll leave this with you. Happy to chat whenever."

Rule: Never follow up more than 4 times without a response. Move on.
