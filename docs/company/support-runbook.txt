Bynle Support Runbook

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
SUPPORT CHANNELS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

- Email (primary) — respond within 24 hours (business days)
- Phone (Premium tier only) — same day
- Critical (widget down) — within 2 hours

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMON ISSUES & RESPONSES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. "The chat widget isn't showing"
----------------------------------------------
Check:
- Is the snippet still in their HTML?
- Did they update their site/theme recently?
- JS error blocking it? (browser console)
- Is the tenant active?

Fix: Re-send snippet, check for script conflicts, test in incognito.

Response: "Thanks for flagging this. I'm looking into it now and will have it sorted within [X hours]."

2. "The AI gave a wrong answer"
----------------------------------------------
Check: What was asked? What was answered? Is the correct info in the knowledge base?

Fix: Update knowledge base, test, notify client.

Response: "Thanks for catching that — I've updated the answer and it's live now."

3. "I'm not getting lead notifications"
----------------------------------------------
Check: Correct email in settings? Spam folder? Email service logs?

Fix: Correct email, resend test notification.

Response: "Let me check the settings. Can you also check your spam folder? Sending a test now."

4. "Can we change the FAQs?"
----------------------------------------------
Process: Client sends updated info → update within 24 hours → confirm.

Response: "Of course — send me the updated info and I'll have it live within 24 hours."

5. "We want to cancel"
----------------------------------------------
Process: Ask why (this is feedback). If fixable, offer to fix. If not, process professionally. Remove widget, deactivate tenant, send confirmation.

Response: "I'm sorry to hear that. Can I ask what's not working? If it's not the right fit, I completely understand."

6. "The widget looks wrong"
----------------------------------------------
Check: Brand colours applied? Logo rendering? CSS conflict?

Fix: Adjust styling, test across browsers.

Response: "I'll take a look. Can you send me a screenshot of what you're seeing?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
SEVERITY LEVELS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Low (FAQ updates, styling tweaks): within 24 hours
Medium (wrong answers, notification issues): within 12 hours
High (widget down, client threatening to cancel): within 2 hours
