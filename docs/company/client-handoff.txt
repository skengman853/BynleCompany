Bynle Client Handoff Guide

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

What the client sees, what they need to do, and what "done" looks like from their perspective.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THE CLIENT NEEDS TO PROVIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Required:
- Clinic name
- Website URL
- Booking link or system name
- Contact email for lead notifications
- Logo
- Top FAQs (or permission for us to write them)
- Services list
- Opening hours

Optional:
- Brand colour hex codes
- Preferred tone of voice
- Specific phrases to use or avoid

Format: Simple form. 15 minutes max.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THE CLIENT SEES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Chat widget appears on their website (bottom-right)
2. Visitors ask questions and get instant answers
3. Widget captures visitor details (name, email, phone)
4. Client gets an email for every new lead
5. Visitors can book directly through chat (if integrated)

That's it. No logins, no dashboards (unless Premium), no complexity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THE CLIENT DOES NOT NEED TO DO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

- No software to install
- No training required
- No daily management
- No content to write (we handle it)
- No technical knowledge needed

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT "DONE" LOOKS LIKE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

[ ] Widget is live on their website
[ ] AI answers their top FAQs correctly
[ ] Lead capture sends notifications to their email
[ ] Booking integration works (if applicable)
[ ] Client has confirmed they're happy with a test conversation
[ ] Client knows how to reach us for support
