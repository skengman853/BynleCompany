Bynle for Accountants — Product Ideas

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
WHY ACCOUNTANTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

- Higher willingness to pay (they understand ROI, bill €150-300/hr)
- Longer contracts (clients don't switch accountants easily)
- Seasonal pain is extreme (January–October is chaos)
- Strong referral networks (they talk to each other)
- Document chasing alone is worth €200-500/month to solve

Timing: Approach in September–October, before tax season panic starts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
CORE PAIN POINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Answering the same client questions hundreds of times a year
2. Phone ringing non-stop during tax season
3. Chasing clients for documents (P60s, receipts, bank statements)
4. New client onboarding is slow manual back-and-forth
5. Missing deadlines or manually tracking them per client
6. Wasting billable hours on admin that earns nothing
7. No time for marketing or new client acquisition

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PRODUCT IDEAS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. AI Tax FAQ Chatbot (already built)
----------------------------------------------
What: Website chatbot trained on Irish tax FAQs and the firm's specific services.
Answers: "When is the deadline?", "What expenses can I claim?", "Do I need to file?", "What are your fees?"
Value: Stops the same 20 questions being asked hundreds of times.
Effort: Low — reuse existing Bynle Assist product.
Price: €99-179/month.

2. Client Document Collection Portal
----------------------------------------------
What: AI-powered intake that tells clients exactly what to upload, checks what's missing, and chases them automatically.
Flow:
  - Accountant triggers "document request" for a client
  - Client gets a link with a checklist (P60, bank statements, receipts, etc.)
  - AI checks uploads and flags missing/wrong items
  - Auto-reminder emails if client hasn't completed in 3/7/14 days
  - Accountant gets notified when everything's in
Value: Accountants spend weeks chasing documents. This does it automatically.
Effort: Medium — file upload + checklist logic + reminder emails.
Price: €200-400/month or €3k-5k implementation.

3. New Client Onboarding Flow
----------------------------------------------
What: AI chatbot qualifies leads and collects structured info before the first call.
Questions:
  - Are you self-employed, PAYE, or a company?
  - What's your annual turnover?
  - Do you have employees?
  - Are your books up to date?
  - What services do you need? (tax returns, bookkeeping, payroll, etc.)
Output: Structured brief sent to the accountant — ready for a 10-minute call instead of a 30-minute discovery.
Value: Saves 20+ minutes per new client. Filters tyre-kickers.
Effort: Low-medium — conversation flow + structured output.
Price: Included in chatbot tier or €500 setup.

4. Deadline Reminder System
----------------------------------------------
What: Auto-email clients before key tax dates.
Dates:
  - Preliminary tax (31 Oct / 15 Nov ROS)
  - Income tax return (31 Oct / 15 Nov ROS)
  - Corporation tax (9 months after year-end)
  - VAT returns (bi-monthly/quarterly)
  - Employer PAYE returns (monthly)
Flow:
  - Accountant sets client type and relevant deadlines
  - System sends reminders at 30/14/7/1 days before
  - Client gets a clear email: "Your tax return is due in 14 days. Here's what we need from you."
Value: No more missed deadlines. No more manual tracking.
Effort: Low — cron job + email templates + tenant-scoped calendar.
Price: €50-100/month add-on.

5. AI Email Triage
----------------------------------------------
What: Scan the firm's inbox, categorise emails, draft responses.
Categories:
  - New enquiry → route to onboarding flow
  - Document submission → attach to client file
  - Routine question → draft AI response for approval
  - Urgent → flag immediately
Flow:
  - Connect via IMAP or forwarding rule
  - AI classifies and drafts
  - Accountant reviews and sends with one click
Value: 50+ emails/day during busy season. Most are routine.
Effort: Medium-high — email integration + classification + drafting.
Price: €300-500/month or €5k-8k implementation.

6. Fee Quote Calculator
----------------------------------------------
What: Website widget where prospects answer 5 questions and get an estimated fee range.
Questions:
  - What type of entity? (sole trader, company, partnership)
  - Annual turnover range?
  - Number of employees?
  - Services needed?
  - Current state of books?
Output: "Based on your answers, our estimated annual fee is €X–€Y. Want to book a call?"
Value: Filters out people who can't afford the firm. Qualifies leads before they call.
Effort: Low — form + pricing rules + lead capture.
Price: Included in chatbot or €300 setup.

7. Client Portal with AI Assistant
----------------------------------------------
What: Branded portal where clients can log in, see their deadlines, upload documents, and ask questions.
Features:
  - Personal dashboard per client
  - Document upload with status tracking
  - AI assistant trained on their specific situation
  - Deadline calendar
  - Secure messaging with the accountant
Value: Professional, modern experience. Differentiates the firm.
Effort: High — but builds on existing multi-tenant system.
Price: €400-600/month or €8k-12k implementation.
